# Voom Medical Devices

> Voom Medical Devices, Inc. is an orthopedic medical device and surgical innovation company in
> Brentwood, Tennessee, founded by bunion surgeon Dr. Neal Blitz. It makes implants and procedural
> systems for minimally invasive bunion surgery (MIBS) and foot-and-ankle reconstruction.

## Products

- [Revcon Screw System](https://www.voommedicaldevices.com/revcon-screw): patented non-compression, dual-zone, single-screw bone fixation system for minimally invasive bunion surgery.
- [Bunionplasty 360 Bunion Repair](https://www.voommedicaldevices.com/bunionplasty): the company's fusion-free minimally invasive bunion repair procedure, intended to preserve range of motion.
- [Bunionplasty.com](https://www.bunionplasty.com/): patient-facing procedure site with a before/after gallery and a doctor locator.
